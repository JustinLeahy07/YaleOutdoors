Gear

Borrow what you need for a few dollars a day.

How renting works

  1. Pick your pickup and return dates below to see what is free, then add what you need.
  2. Send the request. The Gear Manager looks it over and approves it by email. Nothing is held for you until then.
  3. Pay the rental before you take the gear, and a refundable deposit for each item.
  4. Bring it back dry and in working order. You get your deposit back.

If money is tight, tell the Gear Manager or the Treasurers. Financial aid is available.
