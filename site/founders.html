The Tree

For the people who built the club we get to use.

Most of what Yale Outdoors is today was set up by people who graduated years ago, often with very little to go on. This page is a quiet thank-you to a few of them. It is not linked from the front of the site. You found it, so you are part of the club.

Spencer Gray

Treasurer, Yale Mountaineering Club President · 2007 to 2009

Spencer led trips into the White Mountains and the Green Mountains, and wrote up what he learned for whoever came next. In 2009 he did the thing that still shapes the club: he created the Prospectus, a hundred-page case to Yale for coordinated, properly supported outdoor life. It argued for the umbrella that Yale Outdoors became, set out what peer universities do for their outing clubs, and made a plan for a Winter School. Much of what we know about those years is in it.

Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins

Co-Coordinator, YMC Executive Committee · 2008 to 2010

JKT started the liaison program in 2009 to bring graduate and professional students into Yale Outdoors. In the spring of 2010 he arranged professional discounts for the club with Scarpa, Gregory and Marmot. He helped Spencer make the Prospectus, and his notes from 2010 on how the club runs, such as how to reserve a cabin, are still in the institutional knowledge handed to every Co-Coordinator.

Everyone else

The roll on the History page is only the Board members we have on record. Behind every name are the Leaders who drove vans, carried the extra stove, counted heads at the trailhead, and said yes when a first-year asked if they could come. The 1997 founders who brought the club back, and the Leaders of the 1970s before them, are part of this too.

If there is a name that belongs on this page, write to us at yaleoutdoors@gmail.com.
