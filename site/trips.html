Trips

Day trips most weeks, three break expeditions a year, and room for your idea.

Weekend trips

Most weeks in term there is something to join: a hike up Sleeping Giant, East Rock or West Rock, a paddle on the Mill River, a beach bonfire, a farm visit. They are planned by the Weekend Coordinators and the Leaders, announced in the weekly newsletter, and open to everyone. Nobody is expected to know anything beforehand. Beginners are the point.

Coming up

Break expeditions

Three times a year the club goes further: backpacking trips over October, Winter and Spring Break, often to National Parks. They are the biggest thing we do. Details go out in the newsletter, and applications open there first. Leaders and Pnuckles get priority on sign-ups.

No one should have to skip a trip because of a financial burden. Trips are budgeted by the Treasurers and funded in part by Yale grants, and financial aid is available.

Winter School Coming soon

Winter School is our plan for February: a month of evening sessions that teach how to enjoy the outdoors safely in winter, followed each weekend by a trip that puts the lesson to work. It is modeled on the winter schools run by older outing clubs like MIT’s, and the idea goes back to a 2009 proposal by YO and the Yale Mountaineering Club (it is in the Prospectus).

How it would work:

  • Two evenings a week in February. Clothing, food, mountain weather, winter camping, snowshoeing, ice travel, cross-country skiing and more, with each session taught by a different experienced student.
  • A trip every weekend, from easy snowy day hikes up to an overnight, led by two Leaders. Each trip has a learning goal, like building a snow shelter.
  • Gets harder as it goes. Later trips ask that you came to the earlier sessions.
  • All levels welcome. Gear can be borrowed from the gear room.

Dates are not set. Tell us you are interested and we will email you when they are.

Traditions

These come back every year. Dates change, so watch the newsletter for the real ones.

Fall

  • Student Activities FairEarly September. Find our table and sign up.
  • First-Year East Rock HikeThe must-do start to the fall. A Sunday afternoon hike up East Rock for new first-years.
  • Pnuckle sign-upsApplications open at the start of every semester.
  • Sleeping Giant day tripA classic local hike.
  • Beach bonfireAn evening by the water.
  • Kayaking on the Mill RiverAn easy paddle from campus.
  • FallYOThe October Break backpacking trips.
  • Pnuckle retreatOne or two nights for all Pnuckles, at a campground or on a trail.
  • YOHOYale Outdoors Happy Hours for Leaders and members, at least two a semester.
  • Harvard–Yale crunchyfestThe Crunchy Mixer during The Game, with the Harvard Outing Club and other outdoors groups, plus a YOHOHO with Harvard.
  • Pnuckle initiationThe end of the semester, when Pnuckles become Leaders.
  • YOHOHOHOThe December celebration.

Winter

  • WinterYOThe Winter Break trip, after New Year’s and before spring classes. Recent trips went to the Channel Islands in California.
  • Polar PlungeSince 2010, every January or February at Lighthouse Point. We arrange the rides and the warm drinks after.
  • Pnuckle sign-upsOpen again for the spring.

Spring

  • SpringYOThe Spring Break trips, with more room to pick far-off places as the weather warms.
  • Bulldog DaysApril, when admitted students visit. Find our table.

  • Board electionsActive Leaders vote. The spring Co-Coordinator is chosen by the second Saturday of April.
  • Pnuckle initiationThe end of the semester, again.

All year

Suggest a trip

Have somewhere you want to go? The more you tell us, the faster it can happen: where, when, what gear it needs, and how people would get there. The Expedition and Weekend Coordinators read every one, and you do not need to lead it yourself.
