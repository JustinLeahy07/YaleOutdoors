Offering trips, events, and good-spirited community since who knows when. Join us and get outside!

Yale Outdoors is the university’s outing club. We lead day trips from New Haven and bigger trips over October, Winter and Spring Break, rent camping gear for a few dollars a day, and teach one another outdoor skills. You do not need experience. Most people who come the first time have never done it.
