The 2009 Prospectus

A hundred-page case for outdoor life at Yale.

In 2009 Spencer Gray, with help from Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins, wrote the Prospectus to persuade the university to support its outing clubs. It was written when Yale Outdoors was being reorganized as an umbrella for outdoor life at Yale, and it remains the most complete record of how the clubs worked then and what they hoped for.

It is a historical document. The club has changed since, and the constitution is the authority on how things run now.
